# Notice

defog adapts ideas and mechanisms from the following MIT-licensed projects and public writing. None of these authors were involved in, or have endorsed, this project.

- Matt Pocock — https://github.com/mattpocock/skills (MIT). Adapted: the `grilling` interview loop (one question at a time, facts-vs-decisions split, recommended answers, no action before shared understanding) and the `writing-great-skills` principles this repo is written by.
- Thariq Shihipar — "A Field Guide to Claude Fable 5: Finding Your Unknowns" (claude.com blog). Adapted: the map/territory framing and the four-quadrant unknowns taxonomy.
- Neeeophytee — https://github.com/Neeeophytee/finding-unknowns-skills (MIT). Adapted: belief-labeled prototype variations, the reaction-to-criterion sentence, blast-radius question ordering, honest quiz grading.
- Nico Bailon — https://github.com/nicobailon/grill-for-unknowns (MIT). Adapted: the unknowns-ledger concept, the question budget, the fatigue valve, and the material/grounded/answerable question gates.

What defog adds: the ledger as the single shared state through which small skills compose (composable AND stateful); every open unknown carrying its cheapest resolver; visible burn-down as the planning completion criterion; and a merge-gate quiz generated from the ledger rather than from the diff.
